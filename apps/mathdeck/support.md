## Frequently asked questions

### Why does MathDeck need internet?

Only to update currency exchange rates on the Currency tab of
Unit Conversion. Nothing about you is sent. See the
[Privacy Policy](../privacy/#when-mathdeck-uses-the-internet) for
the full story.

### Where is my calculation history stored?

On your phone only. MathDeck keeps your latest 50 calculations,
and nothing is uploaded or synced to us. If your phone's Android
backup is on, your history goes into that backup, which belongs to
you. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes it from
your phone.

### How do I change which calculators I see?

Open **Settings → Profile** and pick Pro, Simple, or Custom.
The Custom option lets you choose exactly which calculators
appear on the home screen.

### What is Tasks mode?

MathDeck includes a simple Tasks (to-do) list alongside the
calculator. Add tasks, check them off, and use the built-in
per-task stopwatch to track how long each one takes. Switch
between Calculator and Tasks from the top-right icon, or swipe.
Your tasks are stored on your phone only and never uploaded.

### Can I get back a task I deleted?

Yes, for 30 days. Tasks you delete wait in **Recently deleted**,
on the Tasks screen, so you can restore them. After 30 days they
are removed for good.

### How do I add the home-screen widgets?

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, choose
**Widgets**, and look for **MathDeck**. There are two: a
calculator shortcut widget and a today's-tasks widget with
quick-add. Both show only data already on your phone. See the
[Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

### The currency rates look old

Open the Currency tab in Unit Conversion, make sure you have an
internet connection, and tap **Refresh**. MathDeck also fetches new
rates by itself when the saved ones are more than 12 hours old. If
a refresh fails, the app keeps using the last rates it had.

### Will my history and tasks move to a new phone?

Yes, if your phone's Android backup is on, or when you copy your
data across while setting up the new phone. Your history,
favorites, tasks and settings move with it; the saved exchange
rates do not, and MathDeck fetches fresh ones.

### How do I ask what data you hold about me?

Email [visualtales8@gmail.com](mailto:visualtales8@gmail.com).
MathDeck sends us no personal data, so we hold none, and we
confirm that in writing within 30 days.

### Can I suggest a new calculator?

Yes, please do. Email us with what you'd like and how you'd
use it.
