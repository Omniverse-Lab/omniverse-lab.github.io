## What's included

### Calculator, with 16+ modes

- **Core:** Basic, Scientific, Date & Time
- **Domain:** Accounting, Civil, Electrical, Physics, Business
- **Visualization:** Graph Plotter, 3D Shapes, Math & Stats
- **Lifestyle:** Health & Fitness, Unit Conversion, Currency Converter

Choose the calculators you see with a profile (Pro, Simple or Custom),
pin your favorites, and come back to your latest 50 calculations in
your history.

### Tasks

A simple to-do list with a built-in stopwatch for every task,
organised by Today, Yesterday, Tomorrow and Archive. Switch between
Calculator and Tasks at any time. Deleted tasks wait in **Recently
deleted** for 30 days, so you can bring them back.

### Home-screen widgets

Calculator shortcuts, and a today's-tasks widget with quick-add.
Both show only what is already on your phone.

### Light and dark

A retro LCD look in both light and dark themes, with optional sound
and haptic feedback on every key.

---

## Your data

Everything MathDeck keeps stays on your phone, and in your own
Android backup if you use one. There is no sign-up, no advertising
and no tracking. MathDeck goes online only to fetch currency exchange
rates, and that request carries nothing about you. The
[privacy policy](privacy/) has the details, and the summary above is
taken straight from it.

---

## Permissions

- **Internet**, so the Currency converter can update exchange rates.
  Used for nothing else.
- **Vibration**, for the small haptic click when you tap a key. You
  can turn it off in Settings.

MathDeck asks for nothing else: not your location, camera,
microphone, contacts or files.
