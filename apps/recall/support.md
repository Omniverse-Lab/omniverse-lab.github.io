## Contact

Email [visualtales8@gmail.com](mailto:visualtales8@gmail.com).
We try to reply within two business days.

When reporting an issue, it helps if you mention:

- Your phone model
- Your Android version
- The ReCall version (shown on ReCall's App info page in your
  phone's settings)
- Whether ReCall is your default phone app
- What you saw and what you expected to see

Please leave out real phone numbers and contact names; we don't
need them to help.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I make ReCall my default phone app?

Open ReCall and tap **Set as default phone app**, then choose
ReCall in Android's dialog. If you declined, tap **Try again**,
or **Open Default apps** and choose ReCall as the **Phone app**.
You can also do it from your phone's settings:
**Settings → Apps → Default apps → Phone app** (the names vary a
little between phone makers).

ReCall works only as your default phone app. Until it is, it
shows no calls and cannot place them. It needs Android 10 or
later, on a phone that can make calls.

### Why doesn't ReCall ask for permissions?

Android grants the call log, contacts, phone and notification
permissions when you make ReCall your default phone app, so
there is nothing else to accept. If you later turn one off in
Android's settings while ReCall is still your phone app, ReCall
shows a card that asks for it again. The
[overview](../) lists what each permission is for.

### How do I switch back to another phone app?

Open **Settings → Apps → Default apps → Phone app** on your phone
and choose the other app. In ReCall, the **Default phone app**
row in Settings (the gear in the top bar) opens Android's Default
apps screen for you.

### What is deleted when I switch back?

ReCall stops reading your calls and deletes everything it stored
from them: its copy of your call log and contacts, your notes,
labels and favorites, its activity journal and any weekly recap
image. If ReCall isn't running when you switch, it deletes them
the next time it starts.

Your call log and contacts belong to Android and stay where they
are, so your other phone app shows them. ReCall keeps its
settings and profile. If you make ReCall your phone app again,
it reads your call log again, but your old notes and favorites
don't come back.

### How do I delete ReCall's data?

In ReCall, **Settings → Data & privacy → Delete all ReCall data**
deletes ReCall's notes, favorites and activity, its copy of
your calls, its cached photos and recap images, and resets its
settings and profile. Your phone's call log and contacts stay. While ReCall is
still your phone app, it then reads your call log again to show
your recents. Uninstalling ReCall also deletes all of its data.

### How do I delete calls from my call history?

- **One row:** long-press it in Recents and tap **Delete**.
- **Every call with one number:** open its call details and tap
  **Delete call history**.
- **Everything:** in ReCall, **Settings → Data & privacy → Clear
  call history**.

These remove the calls from your phone's call log, for every
app, not only from ReCall. They can't be undone.

### How do I block or unblock a number?

Long-press the call in Recents, or open the number's call
details, and tap **Block number** or **Unblock number**. ReCall
uses Android's own blocked-numbers list; in ReCall,
**Settings → Blocked numbers** opens that list so you can review
it. The list belongs
to Android, so switching phone apps or using **Delete all ReCall
data** doesn't change it.

### How does ReCall handle emergency calls?

ReCall places every call, emergency numbers included, through
Android's calling service (Telecom), the same way the phone app
that came with your phone does. ReCall can place calls only while
it is your default phone app and has its Phone permission.
ReCall does not replace Android's own emergency calling screen,
such as the **Emergency call** button on your lock screen, which
you can use instead.

### How do I use Bluetooth, a headset or the speaker during a call?

During a call, tap **Speaker** to switch the loudspeaker on or
off, or tap **Audio** to choose where the call plays: the phone's
earpiece, the speaker, a wired headset or a connected Bluetooth
device. **Audio** appears when there is more than one place the
call can play. On Android 14 and later, Bluetooth devices usually
appear by name. On Android 10 to 13 they appear as "Bluetooth"
("Bluetooth 1", "Bluetooth 2" when several are connected),
because reading their names there would need a Bluetooth
permission ReCall does not ask for. While the call plays
through the earpiece, the screen turns off when you hold the
phone to your ear.

### How do missed-call notifications work?

ReCall posts its own missed-call notification. For a single
caller it offers **Call back** and **Message**, and tapping it
opens Recents on the Missed filter. Opening Recents or swiping
the notification away marks those calls as seen. On a lock
screen that hides sensitive notification content, it shows only
how many calls you missed.

If you don't get them, check that ReCall is your default phone
app, that its Phone and Notifications permissions are on, and
that its **Missed calls** notifications are allowed
(**Settings → Apps → ReCall** in your phone's settings). If you
turned off ReCall's Call logs or Contacts permission, the
notification shows only a count, with no name, number or
buttons, until you allow it again.

### Incoming calls show only as a notification

While you are using your phone, an incoming call shows as a
notification at the top of the screen; that is normal. If calls
never fill the screen, even on the lock screen, full-screen calls
may be off: on Android 14 and later you can turn them off for
each app. In ReCall, **Settings → Full-screen calls** opens
Android's setting so you can turn them back on.

### Is my call data uploaded anywhere?

No. ReCall has no internet access, so it cannot send anything
over the internet, and everything it stores stays on your phone.
Something leaves ReCall only when you choose: a single number you
copy, message, call back or save to your contacts, or your weekly
recap image when you tap **Share weekly recap**. The recap holds
only your own totals and, if you set one, your own profile name
and photo, never a contact's name, number or photo. ReCall has no
export. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for the full story.

### Why are some calls missing, or shown as numbers only?

ReCall shows what is in your phone's call log, which Android or
your phone maker may trim after a certain age or count. A caller
appears as a number when no contact has that number. To save
it, long-press the call in Recents, or open its call details,
and tap **Create new contact** or **Add to existing contact**.

### I updated from an earlier version and my notes are gone

Versions before 1.1.0 were a call-statistics app. ReCall now
keeps nothing from your calls unless it is your phone app, so the
first time 1.1.0 starts it deletes the notes and favorites saved
by earlier versions. Your call history stays in Android and
appears again once ReCall is your phone app.

### How do I change the theme or edit my profile?

The theme button in the top bar switches between light and dark.
Your profile (name, date of birth and photo) is at the top of
Settings. It is entirely optional and, like everything else,
stays on your phone.
