## Frequently asked questions

### Why does ReCall need the Call log and Contacts permissions?

The call log is where your call history lives — without it
there is nothing to analyze. Contacts access lets ReCall show
names instead of bare phone numbers. Both are requested on the
onboarding screen, and everything is processed on your phone.
See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for the full story.

### Is my call data uploaded anywhere?

No. ReCall has no internet access at all — the app cannot make
network requests, so it is technically incapable of uploading
anything. All statistics are computed on your phone and stay
there. Data only leaves the device if you explicitly export a
CSV or share a summary yourself.

### How do I export my stats as a CSV?

Open **Settings → Data & privacy** and choose the CSV export
option. Android then asks where to send the file, and you pick
the app or destination. Nothing is exported unless you do this
yourself.

### How do I delete my data?

Everything ReCall stores lives on your phone. Clearing the
app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app
removes it all. Note this only deletes ReCall's statistics and
settings — your actual call log belongs to Android and is not
touched.

### Why are some calls missing, or shown as numbers only?

ReCall can only see what is in your device's call log, which
Android or your phone maker may trim after a certain age or
count. A caller appears as a number when there is no matching
entry in your contacts, or when Contacts permission has not
been granted.

### Does ReCall work without granting permissions?

Not really. Without Call log access there is no data to
analyze, so the app stays on the onboarding screen with a
clear path to enable access in system settings. You can revoke
either permission at any time in Android settings.

### How do I change the theme or edit my profile?

Open **Settings** for the System / Light / Dark theme switch
and an optional profile (name, date of birth, photo). The
profile is entirely optional and, like everything else, stays
on your phone.
