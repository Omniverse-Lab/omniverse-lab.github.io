Caller names is the only part of ReCall that stores anything
about you on a server: your listed phone number, the name you
chose, your account, any reports you made, and your daily lookup
counts. Everything else ReCall keeps stays on your phone.

---

## Delete it in ReCall

1. Open ReCall and tap the gear (**Settings**).
2. Tap **Caller names**, then **Turn off and delete**, and
   confirm.

ReCall clears the names it saved on your phone at once, then
deletes your listing, your account, your reports and your lookup
counts from our server, and signs you out. Without a connection it
shows "Deleting… we will finish when you are online" and finishes
the next time it starts with a connection.

**Settings → Data & privacy → Delete all ReCall data** does the
same, together with everything ReCall stored on your phone.

## Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have ReCall, for example after uninstalling it,
email [visualtales8@gmail.com](mailto:visualtales8@gmail.com) with
the subject "Delete my Caller names data" and the phone number you
listed. We confirm that the number is yours, then delete the
listing, the account, your reports and your lookup counts within
30 days, and reply when it is done.

Uninstalling ReCall does not delete your listing by itself.

---

## What is not kept anyway

The server never keeps the numbers ReCall looks up for you, and
your contacts and call history never leave your phone. Daily lookup
counts usually delete themselves within two days. Google Cloud's
request logs (time, IP address and user agent) expire after 30 days.
