## What ReCall does

- **Headline stats** — total calls, total talk time, average call length, and a ranked list of your most-called contacts.
- **Time patterns** — time-of-day histograms and a weekday-by-hour heatmap showing when you're on the phone.
- **Trends and distributions** — call-volume trends over time and a breakdown of how long your calls usually run.
- **Per-contact detail** — tap any contact to see their own stats and calling patterns.
- **Smart insights** — auto-generated highlights like your busiest hour or the person you call most.
- **Export on your terms** — save your stats as a CSV file or share a text summary, only when you choose to. ReCall itself never sends anything anywhere.

All analytics are computed on your phone — ReCall has no internet access and works fully offline. No accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
