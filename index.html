How we build

Small, reliable software that helps people get something done, without asking for more than it needs.

  • Privacy first

    We avoid collecting personal data unless an app truly needs it. When an app keeps something, its privacy policy says exactly what, where and for how long.

  • Plain-language policies

    Every app has a privacy policy, terms of use and support page at the same addresses, written for people, with a one-screen summary at the top and a dated history of every change.

  • Focused tools

    Practical apps with one clear job, such as calculating or calling, that are useful the moment you open them.

Questions, support or a privacy request?

A person reads every email and replies, usually within two business days. For help with an app, say which app and version you use.
