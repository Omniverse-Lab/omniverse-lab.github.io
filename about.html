We build focused tools for everyday calculation, organisation and communication, guided by one idea: useful software should be easy to understand, respectful of the person using it, and easy to trust.

What we make

Apps with one clear job each, that work without an account and without handing over data.

  • Utility apps

    Tools that help people calculate, convert, track, plan and finish everyday tasks.

  • Everyday essentials

    Apps for things you do every day, such as making calls, built to keep what they handle on your phone.

  • Productivity companions

    Small workflows that remove friction without asking you to create an account or hand over data.

Every app explains itself

If an app needs permissions, internet access, an account or a server, its documents say so plainly, before anyone has to ask.

An overview
What the app does and what it needs to work.
A privacy policy
What the app keeps, where, for how long and who else handles it, led by a one-screen summary and followed by a dated history of every change.
Terms of use
The agreement for using the app, in plain language wherever the law allows.
A support page
Searchable answers, and a person to email when they are not enough.
A way to delete your data
In the app, and on the web for anything an app keeps on a server.
