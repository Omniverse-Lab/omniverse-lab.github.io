The same documents for every app

Each app's pages live at predictable addresses, so a link in an app store listing or a review keeps working.

Overview
/apps/app-name/
What the app does, what it needs to work, and its privacy summary.
Privacy policy
/apps/app-name/privacy/
What the app keeps, where, for how long, and who else handles it, with a one-screen summary at the top and a dated history of changes.
Terms of use
/apps/app-name/terms/
The agreement for using the app.
Support
/apps/app-name/support/
Answers to common questions, searchable, and how to reach a person.
Delete your data
/apps/app-name/delete/
For apps that keep anything on a server: how to delete it, with or without the app.
