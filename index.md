<section class="section-band" aria-labelledby="goal-title">
	<p class="eyebrow">Goal</p>
	<h2 id="goal-title">Make useful apps that respect the person using them.</h2>
	<p>Our goal is simple: ship small, reliable software that helps people get something done without accounts, ads, unnecessary permissions, or hidden data collection. The company site exists to make each app's policies easy to find and easy to understand.</p>
</section>

<section class="feature-list" aria-labelledby="principles-title">
	<p class="eyebrow">How we build</p>
	<h2 id="principles-title">Operating principles</h2>
	<div class="feature-columns">
		<article>
			<h3>Privacy first</h3>
			<p>We avoid collecting personal data unless an app truly needs it. When an app stores information, it should be clear where that data lives.</p>
		</article>
		<article>
			<h3>Plain-language policies</h3>
			<p>Every app has its own privacy policy, terms of use, and support page at predictable URLs for users and store review teams.</p>
		</article>
		<article>
			<h3>Focused utility</h3>
			<p>We favor practical tools with direct workflows: calculators, education helpers, productivity utilities, and companion apps.</p>
		</article>
	</div>
</section>
